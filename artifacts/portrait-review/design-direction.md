- Work: the project frame opens as the viewer approaches, with a gentle image move. The project index remains keyboard operable.
- Systems: interactive nodes disclose architecture details; the decision sequence uses a paired editorial grid.
- Experience: scroll-linked career rail, expandable role details, education, certifications and awards.
- Closing: separate dusk portrait, line reveals, magnetic email action and functional contact links.
- Reduced motion: all content remains visible; smooth scrolling and decorative film playback are disabled.
